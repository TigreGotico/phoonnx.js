phoonnx.js

In-browser VITS TTS — no server, no API, fully private.

How it works: the voice model is downloaded once from HuggingFace and cached in your browser — later runs are instant and fully offline. Speech is synthesized with onnxruntime-web (WebGPU if your browser supports it, WASM otherwise).

eSpeak voices are piper / Home Assistant compatible — the same .onnx drops straight into the piper add-on.
